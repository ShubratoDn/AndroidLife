**PhoneDeck turns your Android phone into a Bluetooth steering wheel for Euro Truck Simulator 2, a
wireless keyboard, mouse and presentation remote, plus Wi-Fi file transfer and 15+ phone tools.
No software to install on the PC.**

## What's in 1.0.0

### 🚚 Steering wheel for Euro Truck Simulator 2
Steering wheel (360°–1800°) or tilt steering, analog gas and brake with adjustable brake strength,
vertical shifter beside the pedals, retarder, cruise control, every cockpit switch, look pad (tap for
the interior camera), horn and optional CB radio. The PC sees a standard Bluetooth game controller.

### ⌨️ Keyboard, mouse and presentation remote
Keyboard + Touchpad, full keyboard, num pad, a Presentation Remote with laser and **air pointer**,
and **Type on PC** for passwords and long text.

### 📁 Phone ⇄ PC over Wi-Fi
**File Transfer** from any browser (files, folders, text, PC to PC), **clipboard sync**, and
**Live View** of the phone camera or screen. Every new browser must be approved on the phone.

### 📱 Phone tools
Device and battery info, charging meter, storage analyzer, 12 hardware tests, screen time, GPS
speedometer with routes on a map, night screen, QR scanner and maker, and a charging animation for
rooted HyperOS / MIUI phones.
