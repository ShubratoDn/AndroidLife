# Changelog

All notable changes to PhoneDeck. Download any version from
[Releases](https://github.com/ShubratoDn/PhoneDeck/releases).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-03

The first public release.

### PC control over Bluetooth (no PC software)
- **ETS2 Truck Controller**: steering wheel (360°–1800°) with a redesigned 3-spoke wheel and
  steering arc, tilt steering, analog gas and brake with adjustable brake strength, vertical
  shifter beside the pedals, retarder, cruise control, cockpit switches, look pad (tap for the
  interior camera), horn and optional CB radio
- **Keyboard & mouse**: Keyboard + Touchpad, Keyboard, Num Pad + Touchpad, Keyboard Complete
- **Presentation Remote** with timer, laser pointer and an air pointer (point the phone at the screen)
- **Type on PC**: paste text on the phone and the PC types it out

### Phone ⇄ PC over Wi-Fi (any browser)
- **File Transfer**: send files, folders and text between the phone and PCs, or PC to PC
- **Clipboard sync** between the phone and PCs
- **Live View**: phone camera or screen live in the browser, up to original quality
- Device approval with matching codes, per-device access levels, PIN option, security log
- *Send to PC* in the Android share sheet and a Quick Settings tile for the clipboard

### Phone tools and utilities
- Device Info, Battery Info, Charging Meter, Storage Analyzer, Sensor Tester (12 hardware tests),
  Screen Time
- Speedometer with trip history and routes on an OpenStreetMap map, GPX export
- Night Screen, QR & barcode Scanner, QR & Barcode Maker
- Charging Animation for rooted HyperOS / MIUI phones (LSPosed)
